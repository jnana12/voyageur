Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: December 21, 2024

Since Voyageur AI provides instant digital itinerary generation services, specific conditions apply to cancellations and refunds.

1. Cancellation Policy

Individual Credits: Cancellation is not applicable for individual trip credits once they have been used to generate an itinerary, as the digital service is consumed instantly.

Credit Packs: If you have purchased a credit pack (e.g., Explorer, Voyageur, Nomad) and have not used any credits, you may request a cancellation within 24 hours of the purchase transaction.

2. Refund Policy

We are committed to your satisfaction. Refunds are issued under the following circumstances:

  • Technical Failure: If the Voyageur system fails to generate an itinerary due to a technical error but credits or funds were deducted, an automatic refund of the credit will be issued. If a direct payment was made, a full monetary refund will be processed.
  • Unused Services: Refunds for fully unused credit packs can be requested within 7 days of purchase.

3. Processing Timeline

Approved refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days. The amount will be credited back to the original payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, UPI, etc.) used during the transaction.

4. Contact Us

To request a refund or cancellation, please contact our support team with your Transaction ID:
